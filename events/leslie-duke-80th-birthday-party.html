Happiest of Happy Birthdays, Leslie!

Actual: August 14th, 2026

Party: August 21-23, 2026

She's turning 80,
so it's time to party!

You might be surprised to hear that of all of us, Leslie says that she is the most incredulous about this passage of time. "How can it possibly have been 80 years?!," she says. Well, it has been, and that means it is time to celebrate!

WHERE

Out on the beautiful Oregon Coast in Rockway Beach. The new-and-exciting Duke Family Beach House is finally to a point where we can host larger gatherings, and what better time for a debut!?!

Here is the address for all your mapping needs:

176 South Coral Street
Rockaway Beach, OR 97136

WHEN

Her actual birthday is on Friday, August 14th, 2026, but we will be out there on the Oregon Coast the weekend after that, August 21-23, enjoying the sunshine and cool salty air. We would love for you join us! Come out for the day or stay for the whole weekend. 🤙🏻

FESTIVITIES

There will be lots of unplanned and general beachy hangings-around going on in Rockaway for the whole weekend, but we definitely want to have a few set activities for easier planning and maximization of joy. The biggest, fullest day will be Saturday, August 22nd, so if you're planning on a day trip, make that the day!

  • Friday, August 21st
    • 6pm - Dinner at El Trio Loco Mexican Restaurant. If you will be around for this and want to be included, please shoot us a little RSVP so we can reserve a big enough table at the restaurant.
    • 8pm - Sunset walk along the beach
  • Saturday, August 22nd
    • 9am - Breakfast burritos and coffee at the Beach House
    • MISCELLANEOUS BEACH TIMES!!
    • 6pm - Grilling out at the Beach House
    • 9pm - Bonfire on the beach

    In the in-betweens and the afters, we will be getting into some miscellaneos beach times around town: flying kites, long walks on the beach, thrifting at the Hope Chest, and possibly riding the old-timey choo-choo train!

  • Sunday, August 23rd
    • 9am - Breakfast bagels and coffee at the Beach House

LODGING

Depending on the timing and duration of your stay, there is a decent chance that one of the bunk beds in the beach house bunk room could be available for you! Again, so much of that depends on the timing and duration of your stay, with more available in the befores and afters. If crashing out in a flop house sounds like fun to you, let us know, we can probably figure something out. The more the merrier!

But if that sounds like a bit much or you have little ones or older ones or larger groups or maybe you want a place that has a hot tub or whatever! That's cool! There are quite a few smaller beach hotels and little getaway cottages that offer pretty good places for a pretty good price. Rockaway Beach is small enough that most places will be walkable to the beach house. Google it or AirBnb it or whatever.

We recommend that you book whatever your gonna book ASAP because mid August is peak summer season and stuff will get snatched up. Go forth and be merry!
